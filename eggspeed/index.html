How it works

Plug in. Read everything. Ride.

1Connect

Plug the Bafang programming cable between your phone and the controller over USB OTG - EggSPEED identifies it automatically.

2Read everything

Every Basic, Pedal, and Throttle parameter, plus all 10 assist levels, in one scrollable read-only view - copy it all with one tap.

3Ride with live data

Speed, power, current, voltage, distance, average speed, Wh/km usage, and estimated range - updated live as you ride.

Full configuration

Every parameter, exactly as your controller has it.

Basic (battery protection, current limit, wheel and speed-meter setup), Pedal Assist (sensor type, start current, slow-start mode, work mode), and Throttle (start/end voltage, speed or current mode) - all read directly from the controller and shown the way the factory display would.

Read-only in this release - see and understand your full setup with zero risk of a bad write.

Assist levels

Dial in every level, see the shape of your ride.

Speed limit and current limit for each of the 10 assist levels (0-9), plus power- and speed-distribution charts across all levels at a glance.

See exactly how aggressive level 7 really is compared to level 3 - before you're already riding it.

Battery & accuracy

Range you can actually trust.

Tell EggSPEED your cell count and capacity once, and the cockpit's range estimate blends your riding history with your current riding style - the longer the trip, the more it adapts to how you're riding right now.

Controller reading current wrong after a shunt mod? Calibration applies an app-side current and voltage correction factor - nothing is changed on the controller.

Profiles & settings

Save your setup, take it anywhere.

Save the current configuration as a named profile, export it to a .ini file, or import one back in.

Switch between km/h and mph, set your real odometer reading, or force extreme values with Test mode to check the cockpit layout on your own device.
