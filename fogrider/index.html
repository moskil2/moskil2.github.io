Import in one click

Already tracking rides elsewhere? Bring them all here.

Komoot - log in with your Komoot account directly inside FogRider and pick which recorded activities to pull in, no manual export needed.

Strava - a one-click bookmarklet runs on Strava's own site using your logged-in session, packs your activities into a ZIP, and hands it straight to FogRider.

✓

Local-first, transparent about the rest.

FogRider has no backend server of its own. Your tracks and saved profiles live only on your PC - the app only reaches out to a service when a specific feature needs it, and tells you which one.

✓No account, no upload of your tracks
✓No analytics or tracking
✓Talks to OpenStreetMap only for map data & surface analysis
✓Komoot login stays in your session, never stored by us
