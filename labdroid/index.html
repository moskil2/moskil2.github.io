How it works

Open it. Pick a category. Watch it live.

1Know your device

The Dashboard shows model, manufacturer, Android version and codename, security patch and kernel at a glance.

2Browse every sensor

Every sensor your phone exposes, with its real chip name and a live value - plus sound level and GSM signal.

3Dive into the data

Tap any sensor for a live chart, min/max/average and standard deviation, then export what you recorded.

Hardware

CPU, GPU, memory - the real specs.

CPU core count, core clusters with their clock speeds, ABI, max frequency, hardware platform and SoC.

GPU renderer, vendor, OpenGL ES / GLSL / EGL versions and the number of supported GL extensions - plus memory, storage, display and audio details in the same tab.

Sensors

Every sensor, live, with its real chip name.

Light, barometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, orientation, accelerometer and more - each one shown with the vendor's own sensor name and a live reading.

The detail view plots a live chart, keeps minimum, maximum, average and standard deviation, lets you switch sampling speed (Fast, Normal, Slow), pause, reset or export.

Cameras

What every lens can actually do.

Each camera module listed separately - wide, ultra-wide, telephoto, front - with sensor size, pixel array, focal length, field of view, zoom range, OIS, flash, RAW support, max FPS and logical multi-camera.

GPS & compass

Every satellite above you, live.

A satellite radar shows where each satellite is in the sky, with signal strength per satellite for GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, QZSS, SBAS and IRNSS.

Precise position, altitude, speed, bearing and accuracy, a compass with true-north correction, and track recording with GPX / CSV export.

Battery

Your battery, beyond the percentage.

Level, status, charging source, health and technology, plus the electrical side: temperature, voltage, current, remaining charge, design capacity and cycle count.

Some values, like cycle count, are only reported by newer Android versions or specific devices - LabDroid clearly marks them as unavailable when your phone doesn't provide them.

Settings & permissions

Your phone, your rules.

Dark or Light theme, metric or imperial units, and a default sensor sampling speed - switchable any time.

Every permission LabDroid can use is listed with its current state, so you always see exactly what the app has access to.
