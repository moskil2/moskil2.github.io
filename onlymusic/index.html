FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does OnlyMusic stream music from the internet?

No. OnlyMusic plays the audio files stored on your own phone. The app has no internet access at all.

Which audio files does it play?

Common formats such as MP3, FLAC, OGG, Opus, M4A/AAC and WAV, with their titles, artists, albums and covers.

Does OnlyMusic need access to all my files?

No. You pick the music folders through the Android folder picker, and OnlyMusic reads only those. It asks for no storage permission.

Does it work with Android Auto?

Yes. Your Library, Playlists and Favorites show on the car screen with their covers, and you can pick any track while driving.

Where are my playlists and settings stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy.

Is OnlyMusic free?

Yes. OnlyMusic is free on Google Play, with no ads and nothing locked.
