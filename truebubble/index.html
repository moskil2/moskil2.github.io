Calibration & accuracy

An instrument you can trust.

Place your phone on a known-flat reference and one tap zeroes the sensor offset, with step-by-step on-screen guidance. No guesswork, no drift left uncorrected.

Want more control? Manual calibration lets you fine-tune pitch and roll offsets directly with +/- controls, and always shows exactly when the device was last calibrated.

Make it yours

Readable in any light.

Switch between dark and light themes, or flip on high-contrast bubble mode for better visibility outdoors.

Pick from 9 bubble colors to match your eye, your job site, or just your taste.
